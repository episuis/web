EPISUIS

Applied epidemiology with a systems perspective.

EPISUIS is a technical firm specializing in swine production systems, combining applied epidemiology, data analysis and purpose-built solutions to turn health and production information into evidence for practical decisions.

Purpose Understand health and production problems in context, structure the information needed to address them, and turn that information into strategies, projects and solutions that can work under real production conditions.

What we do

From the problem to a usable solution.

We work across different scales, from an individual production unit to farm groups, production networks, producer organizations, regions and collaborative projects.

The resulting evidence can support vaccination and health strategies, biosecurity and surveillance programs, diagnostic interpretation, risk-factor assessment, production-performance evaluation, spatial characterization and collective interventions.

Depending on the question, the work may involve epidemiological design, sampling, diagnostic and molecular information, statistical and spatial analysis, database structuring, visualization, monitoring and digital tools.

How EPISUIS works

One technical foundation, different ways to apply it.

Working scale

From a single farm to regional systems.

EPISUIS can work with individual production units, farm groups, production networks, producer organizations, institutions, territorial programs and multi-institutional projects.

Scale changes the question, study design and tools required, but not the core principle: understand the system before deciding how to analyze it or intervene.

Connecting the stages is part of the solution.

One of EPISUIS's strengths is the ability to connect stages that are often handled separately: defining the question, designing information collection, structuring capture, integrating diagnostic results, building reliable datasets, analyzing the evidence and interpreting it for decision-making.

This integration makes it possible to think about later analysis from the moment information is generated, preserving context and traceability throughout the process.
