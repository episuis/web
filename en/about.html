EPISUIS

Applied epidemiology with a systems perspective.

EPISUIS is a technical firm specializing in swine production systems, combining applied epidemiology, data analysis and purpose-built solutions to turn health and production information into evidence for practical decisions.

Purpose Understand health and production problems in context and turn dispersed information into decisions, strategies and solutions that can work under real production conditions.

The EPISUIS ecosystem

One technical foundation, three ways to apply it.

Consulting addresses specific questions and builds solutions around context and available evidence.

Vigilancia EPISUIS provides infrastructure for structuring and running epidemiological projects with traceability across field work, samples, laboratory workflows and results.

EPISUIS Production organizes production information, supports ongoing monitoring and generates useful indicators for farm management.
