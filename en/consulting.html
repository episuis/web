Consulting

The problem defines the method.

EPISUIS does not start from predefined packages. Each engagement is structured around the question, system scale, available information and the decisions that need to be supported.

Approach Applied epidemiology to understand health and production problems, generate the information that is actually needed, and turn it into usable decisions, strategies or technical solutions.

What a project may integrate

Health, production, diagnostics, territory and time within the same question.

Design + samplingRisk + biosecurityDiagnostics + sequencesProduction + monitoring

Capabilities

Different tools for different questions.

These are not separate packages. Capabilities are combined according to the problem and can support anything from a focused technical question to a larger multi-unit project.

Epidemiological design and sampling

Population and study-unit definition, sampling strategy, frequency, follow-up and interpretation criteria.

Risk analysis and biosecurity

Associated factors, exposure patterns, critical points and heterogeneity across farms or production systems.

Diagnostics and molecular epidemiology

Integration of PCR, serology, sequence data, phylogeny and other diagnostic information within its epidemiological context.

Production and performance

Integration of production information to identify patterns, variation, critical points and relationships with health outcomes.

Spatial and network analysis

Geographic distribution, links among units, flows, clusters and the territorial structure of health problems.

Data, visualization and applied systems

Data cleaning and structuring, analysis, maps, dashboards and digital workflows when methodology needs to become a reproducible operation.

Process

Design, execution and interpretation within one workflow.

01

Understand

Define the problem, the decisions involved, the population and the scale of work.

02

Design and structure

Determine what information is needed, how it should be generated, how often, and how traceability will be preserved.

03

Execute and analyze

When required, structure capture, follow-up and diagnostics; then integrate the data and apply the appropriate analytical methods.

04

Interpret and transfer

Turn results into conclusions, strategies, protocols, structured datasets, visualizations or tools that can continue to be used.

Decisions we can help support

Epidemiology becomes valuable when it improves decisions.

Animal health

Vaccination strategies, surveillance, biosecurity, diagnostic follow-up and prioritization of health problems.

Production

Identification of critical points, interpretation of performance indicators and assessment of variation across farms or time periods.

Epidemiology

Sampling design, population definition, monitoring frequency and evaluation of patterns or risk factors.

Organizations and regions

Collective health programs, territorial characterization, cluster identification, production networks and regional strategies.

Projects and collaborations

Methodological design, epidemiological operations, information integration and analysis for institutional, multi-unit, national or international projects.
