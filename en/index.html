Working scale

From a single farm to regional systems.

Scale changes the question, study design and tools required. EPISUIS can work with individual production units, farm groups, production networks, organizations, regions and multi-institutional projects.

Production units

Health and production interpretation, critical-point identification and support for farm-level decisions.

Groups and networks

Comparisons among units, relationships, flows, patterns and shared problems.

Organizations and institutions

Surveillance programs, collective strategies, information generation and technical support for organizational decisions.

Regions and projects

Epidemiological characterization, spatial analysis and regional, national or international collaboration.
